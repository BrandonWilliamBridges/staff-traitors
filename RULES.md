# Staff Traitors: player rules

**The idea.** A few of us are secretly Traitors. Everyone else is Faithful. The game runs one school week, Monday to Friday. The Faithful win by banishing every Traitor. The Traitors win by surviving to the end.

**Joining.** It is voluntary. Sign in with your school email and the code from the Game Master. Leave any time with one button; you become a Ghost and keep your points.

**Your role.** Open My Role somewhere private and press and hold. Never show anyone your role screen or a screenshot of it; that removes you from the game.

**Each school day.**
- **Morning:** find out in the app who the Traitors murdered. New missions open. (On Monday the first murder is revealed at the roundtable.)
- **During the day:** missions. They take five to fifteen minutes, before school, at lunch, in planning or after school. Every mission adds to the prize pot. Some earn a secret Shield that stops one murder.
- **Roundtable (lunch or after school):** everyone still in the game casts one secret vote for who they think is a Traitor. The most votes is banished and their role is revealed. A tie means one revote between the tied players; a second tie means nobody goes.
- **Night:** the Traitors choose their next victim.
- **Friday:** the Final Roundtable.

**The end.** At the Final Roundtable, after each banishment the remaining players vote End Game or Banish Again. It ends only if everyone chooses End Game. If only Faithful are left, they share the pot. If a Traitor is still there, the Traitors take it all.

**Hard rules.**
- Never during class time, in front of students, or in student areas.
- Never involve students, student work or student information. No students in photos.
- Lying about your role out loud is part of the game. Touching anyone's real work, room, mail, email, grades or reputation is not.
- Nobody pays anything. The prize is free or donated.
- Away, sick, at a training or on duty? Mark yourself Away. You cannot be murdered while away, and you can vote from anywhere.
- Anything feel wrong? Use Report a Concern. It goes privately to the Game Master and the principal.
- Write everything in the app as if anyone could read it later.
