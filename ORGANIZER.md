# Staff Traitors: organizer guide

## Before the season (two to three weeks ahead)

1. Pick one school week, Monday to Friday, that avoids testing windows, grading deadlines and breaks. Set the start date to the Monday and the end date to the Friday.
2. Start the app, sign in at /admin.html, and fill in Season setup: dates, blackout dates, your school's email domain, bell schedule, departments.
3. Decide the settings: Traitor count (blank is about one per nine players), murders per night (1 for about 15 players, 2 for 18 to 20; see the readme for bigger staffs), evaluators Faithful only (recommended), sealed mode (recommended if you also work at the school), reveal mode (totals only is friendlier).
4. Add the principal as principal sponsor and give them their code. They approve the season in the app.
5. Tick the checklist: staff association or union representative told, district technology office says it is fine, no school systems connected.
6. Choose a free or donated prize the principal approves.
7. Load the one-week plan from the Missions card. Set every quiz answer marked EDIT ME. Print the code cards, and hide each one in its staff-only area the morning its mission opens (Six Card Sweep's six cards go out Monday).

## Launch

1. Open registration. Add staff emails and print the sign-in codes. Hand codes to staff without school email or a phone.
2. Announce at a faculty meeting: one-page rules, the opt-out promise, the prize.
3. When registration closes, press Cast Roles before the weekend so people know their role by Monday. Players see "Your role is ready."

## Each school day

| When | You do |
| --- | --- |
| Monday before school | Start round 1. Open today's planned missions. The Traitors pick their first victim during the day. |
| Tuesday to Friday before school | Process the murder. Open today's planned missions. |
| During the day | Review check-ins. Announce pairs for pair missions. |
| Lunch or after school | (Monday: process the murder first.) Open the roundtable, give people time to talk, close the vote and show the result. Then start the next round so the Traitors vote overnight. |
| Friday | The Final Roundtable: one meeting, a few quick votes, until everyone left votes End Game. |

Only one roundtable runs per school day. A second one the same day needs the override box.

Steps are blocked on weekends and blackout dates. The override box allows one anyway and records it in the audit log.

## Keeping it friendly

- Watch the concerns inbox. Pause the game for testing weeks, a crisis on campus or a loss in the community.
- Mark people Away when they are out, and Left School if they leave.
- If a Traitor opts out, you get a private note. Consider whether the game still feels fair.

## After the season

1. Celebrate and hand out the prize.
2. Let everyone read the season story (every role, every round, the revealed random seed).
3. Download the data if you need it, then delete all player data within 30 days.
